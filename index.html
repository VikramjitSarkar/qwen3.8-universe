First Stars Ignite

Gravity pulls gas into the first giant stars. Inside their cores, the first heavy elements — carbon, oxygen, iron — are forged.

Stars cluster into the first galaxies. Structure emerges from the soup — the scaffolding of the cosmos as we know it.
